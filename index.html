Core Capabilities

One Platform. Every Diagnostic Decision.

The Auto Triage links engine acoustics, dashboard visuals, and driver narratives into one closed-loop pre-diagnostic platform compressing uncertainty before the car enters the bay.

PBUCE Engine
Engine 01

Pre-Booking Uncertainty Compression (PBUCE)

Probabilistic AI inference that maps noisy consumer data against 10,000+ UK vehicle fault patterns to produce confidence-based fault classifications before booking.

Instead of a binary fault code, PBUCE outputs a 75% probability of a sensor fault versus 25% wiring harness failure, compressing pre-booking uncertainty and managing consumer expectations before any physical inspection occurs.
MCWTL Layer
Engine 02

Multimodal Consumer-to-Workshop Translation (MCWTL)

Converts vague driver descriptions "shaking at high speed" into standardised Technician-Ready Objects using FFT audio analysis and CNN visual processing.

NLP models trained on UK automotive terminology transform non-expert narratives into precise diagnostic objects like "Potential warped front brake discs; frequency-matched acoustic signature; safe to drive under 40mph," slashing intake time by 35%.
RSIME Routing
Engine 03

Repair Suitability Intelligence Matching (RSIME)

Routes vehicles to workshops based on technical alignment EV certifications, ADAS equipment, IMI TechSafe credentials not just proximity or price bidding.

In a market where only 22% of technicians are EV-qualified and 2% are ADAS-certified, RSIME ensures complex repairs reach the right workshop first time, eliminating costly no-fix returns and wasted bay hours.
RORLE Learning
Engine 04

Repair Outcome Reconciliation Learning (RORLE)

Every completed repair feeds back into the AI as a verified learning event, reconciling initial triage with final workshop invoices to continuously refine diagnostic accuracy.

This closed-loop framework creates a proprietary, outcome-linked dataset of the UK vehicle parc's real failure modes a technical moat functionally impossible for generalist tech competitors to replicate.
Urgency Scoring
Engine 05

Urgency Scoring & Safe-to-Drive Guidance

Real-time fault severity assessment integrated with the DVSA MOT History API, delivering immediate safety guidance and confidence-based cost banding to motorists.

Benchmarked against DVSA categorical definitions of Dangerous, Major, and Advisory faults, the urgency engine prevents unnecessary vehicle write-offs and protects the 76% of UK drivers vulnerable to unexpected repair bills.
DVSA Integration
Engine 06

DVSA API & Compliance Infrastructure

Deep integration with the official DVSA MOT History API, Privacy-by-Design Edge AI processing, and full UK GDPR / FCA Consumer Duty alignment built in from day one.

AES-256 encryption, ISO/IEC 27001-aligned cybersecurity, and a Compliance-by-Design framework transform regulatory requirements into an institutional trust asset for insurers, fleets, and government bodies.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is the "information gap" The Auto Triage solves?

+
The information gap is the breakdown in communication between a non-technical driver and a specialist workshop. A driver describes "a weird noise" a technician needs a frequency-analysed acoustic signature and a probable fault family to prepare the right bay and parts. The Auto Triage bridges that gap in under two seconds, converting vague symptom descriptions into Technician-Ready diagnostic objects before the car leaves the driveway.

Do drivers or garages need any specialist hardware to use the platform?

+
No. The Auto Triage is entirely hardware-free. Unlike OBD dongle tools (Carly, FIXD), our platform uses Fast Fourier Transform audio analysis and Convolutional Neural Network visual processing on a standard consumer smartphone. No additional sensors, devices, or physical installation are required.

How is The Auto Triage different from a booking marketplace like FixMyCar?

+
Booking marketplaces focus on price comparison for the booking event they don't solve the diagnostic problem before booking occurs. The Auto Triage is a pre-transaction intelligence layer: it identifies what is wrong, how serious it is, what it should cost, and which specific workshop has the certified equipment to fix it all before a booking is ever made.

What results did the Oxford Regional Pilot achieve?

+
The prototype achieved 91% correct fault classification for common powertrain and braking faults, 88% precision in isolating mechanical anomalies from background road noise, sub-1.8 second inference latency on standard smartphones, and a 35% average reduction in initial workshop diagnostic intake time across partner garages in the Oxford and Thames Valley region.

How does The Auto Triage handle data privacy for sensitive vehicle media?

+
The platform uses a Privacy-by-Design Edge AI model where initial signal processing (audio FFT, visual CNN) is performed on the user's device, minimising transmission of raw sensitive media. All data in transit and at rest is encrypted with AES-256. The platform is UK GDPR and DPA 2018 compliant, with anonymisation of all vehicle identifiers (plates, VINs) used in RORLE model training.

How long does integration take for a garage management system?

+
The Auto Triage is built with an API-first architecture. Direct integration with major Garage Management Systems (GMS) and the DVSA MOT History API is available via RESTful and WebSocket APIs. Once a configuration template is established for a GMS type, subsequent deployments typically activate within hours rather than weeks.
